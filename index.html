FAMILY

FAMILY

HUMAN × ANIMAL
There is one part of my life that keeps everything grounded. My hairless cat is my family.

Living with an animal changes the way you understand home, movement, comfort and everyday life.

It reminds me that the best products are not designed around categories. They are designed around relationships.

The way we live together. The way we move together. The way we experience the outdoors together.

Good products are not only about what we use. They are about how we live together.
